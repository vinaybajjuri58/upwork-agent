# Proposal playbook

## Structure
1. **Relevant opening:** show you understood the client's specific desired result.
2. **Implementation plan:** two or three concrete steps, tailored to the job.
3. **Evidence:** one or two directly relevant projects or verifiable work examples.
4. **Assumptions and risks:** mention any scope question that materially affects effort.
5. **Next step:** propose a focused question or a short kickoff discussion.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every claim is supported by verified profile/work/portfolio evidence.
- [ ] Personal projects are labelled as personal projects.
- [ ] All screening questions are answered separately and honestly.
- [ ] Scope and exclusions are clear.
- [ ] Price and timeline are plausible for the described work.
- [ ] No generic flattery, invented client names, unsupported outcomes, or guaranteed results.
- [ ] Complete proposal and current Connects cost are included in the review preview.
- [ ] No submission or boost occurs without fresh explicit approval.

## Pricing
Recommend pricing based on the job's actual scope, urgency, risk, and the user's current profile. Do not force every job into one default price. For fixed-price work, split the scope into deliverables and state assumptions. For hourly work, suggest a rate and a bounded first milestone when appropriate.

## Reuse evidence responsibly
Never claim a portfolio project solves a client's exact production problem unless that is true. Explain what the project demonstrates and where the match is partial.
